Struktur

  1. In-app header (sama): KKD + "Master Data & Recouncil" + Reconciliation Engine + BARCODE CHECKER
  2. Tab strip: Consolidation (non-aktif) | Recouncil (aktif charcoal)
  3. Page header: display title + 2 tombol (Reset + Proses Recouncil terracotta)
  4. 3 upload zone: Opname multi-file (1.4fr) + Master (1fr) + ASPx opsional (1fr). Zone yang sudah dimuat = border + bg hijau pucat. Hover = border terracotta.
  5. Summary 4-kolom: Match (hijau) / Missing (merah) / Extra (amber) / Total
  6. Recouncil table: Barcode + Nama + Master (✓/✗) + Opname (✓/✗) + Status pill

Animasi

  • Upload zone loaded: hijau pucat dengan border hijau, hover = terracotta
  • Row matched: hijau tint, missing: merah, extra: amber (3px strip kiri)
  • Status pill: dot warna solid + label uppercase
  • Tombol Proses Recouncil: terracotta primary dengan lift animation
  • Card lift on hover (220ms)

Hover zone, row, dan tombol. Balas di terminal.
